    How the logic works

    Expected recovery internally = principal still at risk × chance the user pays on their own (by days late; ×0.5 if they paid <10% of installments; ×0.75 with 2+ collection notes; ×1.5 if they paid in the last 30 days; ×0.5 if no payment in 90+ days).
    Expected recovery via the agency = principal at risk × agency recovery rate for the country (Aruba 45%, Curaçao/other 2%) − Fl 250 agency cost.

    1. Keep internal – reminders 0–30 days late.
    2. Small balance less than Fl 1,000 principal at risk (break-even = 250 ÷ (45% − 20%)). Keep chasing; block & write off from day 120.
    3. Keep internal – still paying late, but paid something in the last 30 days.
    4. Keep internal when the agency doesn't come out ahead – e.g. Curaçao, where the agency recovers ~2%.
    5. Send to agency NOW agency comes out ahead and 61+ days late (or 31+ days, > Fl 3,000 and barely paid).
    6. Final notice – agency at day 61 31–60 days and the agency would come out ahead.

    Principal at risk = per open plan, original principal × unpaid installments ÷ total installments. Agency figures come from the Deurwaarder dashboard (only 45% of agency cases ever paid anything; first payment after ~13 months). Cure chances are estimates – adjust them under “Logic levers”.
